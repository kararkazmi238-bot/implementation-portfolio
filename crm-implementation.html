Business case & objectives

HealthBridge Community Services (a mid-size nonprofit healthcare org, 120 staff, Austin TX) was managing client relationships across 3 disconnected systems — a legacy Access database, paper files, and individual Excel spreadsheets. This caused duplicate records, missed follow-ups, and no visibility into case outcomes. The project goal was to consolidate onto Salesforce Nonprofit Success Pack (NPSP) to centralize client data, automate outreach workflows, and enable real-time KPI reporting.

Phase breakdown

1
Discovery & requirements gathering
Stakeholder interviews with case managers, billing, and IT. Current-state process mapping across all 3 legacy systems. Requirements doc produced (functional + non-functional). Data audit — 4,200 client records reviewed for migration quality.
Wks 1–3
2
Configuration & data migration
Salesforce NPSP org setup: custom objects, page layouts, workflow rules, and permission sets. Data cleansing and ETL migration of 4,200 records. 3 sandbox validation cycles before production push. Integration with billing system via API connector.
Wks 4–9
3
UAT & end-user training
52 UAT test cases executed across 3 user groups. 2-week structured training program: 4 role-based sessions (case managers, billing, supervisors, admins). Training materials: user guides, quick-reference cards, recorded walkthroughs. UAT sign-off required before go-live gate.
Wks 10–13
4
Go-live & hypercare support
Phased cutover: case management team first, billing + ops day 3. Legacy system read-only for 2 weeks post go-live. Dedicated hypercare support queue (SLA: 2-hr response). Daily adoption check-ins for first 10 days. 30-day post go-live health review.
Wks 14–16
